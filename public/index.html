HOW IT WORKS

From leftover to delivered, in five steps

Every donation moves through the same simple pipeline, so nothing gets lost and nothing sits until it spoils.

Step 1

List the food

Donor enters the food type, quantity, pickup point and remaining time before it expires.

Step 2

Check safety & priority

The system flags each donation as Safe, Donate Soon, Urgent, or Expired based on dynamic time left.

Step 3

Match an NGO

Available donations are matched with an NGO that can collect and distribute them quickly.

Step 4

Assign & deliver

A volunteer is assigned for pickup, and the delivery is tracked until it's completed.

⏱️

Smart Expiry Priority

Server dynamically computes remaining hours on every request. Food closest to expiry is always pushed to the top.

🔐

Role-Based Access

Admins manage the entire pipeline; Donors, NGOs, and Volunteers access dedicated role portals backed by SQLite and JWT.

📍

End-to-End Tracking

Follow every donation from creation to matching, volunteer assignment and confirmed final delivery.
